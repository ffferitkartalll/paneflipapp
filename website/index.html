PaneFlip

Split your Mac's screens into zones. Drop apps into them. Flip between a zone's apps with one glass pill.

Download for macOS View on GitHub
brew install --cask ffferitkartalll/tap/paneflipapp

Free and open source (MIT). Needs macOS 27.

How it works

Three ideas, and your windows always know where to go.

1

Snap

Drag a window while you hold ⌘. The zones light up; let go over one and the window slides in and fills it. Apps you put in a zone are remembered and return when they reopen.

2

Stack and flip

Several apps can share one zone. Point at it and the glass pill appears: click an app, scroll over the pill, or press ⌃⌥← → to flip.

3

Go beyond the screen

A layout can go on past the edge of the display, with as many zones as you like. Push the pointer to the edge, or hold the keys and scroll with two fingers.

Made for the way you work

Layouts you draw, a pill that stays out of the way, and a Mac that remembers.

Snap

Drag a window into a zone

Hold ⌘ while you drag and the zones light up. Let go over one and the window slides in and fills it. PaneFlip remembers which app belongs where and puts it back when the app reopens.

  • ⌘ hold while dragging Show the zones
  • let go Fill the zone

Stacks

Many apps, one zone

Put more apps in a zone and they stack. A small glass pill appears when you point at it: click an app, scroll over the pill, or use the arrow keys to flip. The next window glides in beside the one it replaces.

  • ⌃⌥→ Next window
  • ⌃⌥← Previous window

Scroll

Scroll through a zone’s windows

Hold ⌥ ⌃ and scroll with two fingers: the windows follow your fingers, pass several at once and settle on the nearest one. A mouse wheel steps one window at a time. The speed is a setting.

  • ⌥⌃ + scroll Through a zone’s windows
  • Two fingers

Beyond the screen

Zones past the edge

A layout can go on past the edge of the display, with as many zones as you like. Push the pointer to the edge, or hold ⌘ ⌥ ⌃ and scroll, and the whole layout slides over.

  • ⌃⌥⌘→ Zones to the right
  • ⌃⌥⌘← Zones to the left
  • ⌘⌥⌃ + scroll Slide the layout
  • Two fingers

Layouts

Draw your own layout

Click to split a zone, drag a line to resize, drag a zone onto its neighbour to merge. Or start from one of twenty common shapes. Name your layouts and zones, and set a gap for each.

  • click Split
  • drag a line Resize
  • drag a zone Merge

Menu bar

Switch layouts in one click

Every desktop of a display can use the layout you choose for it. Pick another from the menu bar and the windows glide to their new zones.

  • Menu bar Choose a layout

And everything around it

The details that make it feel like part of the Mac.

Layouts

  • Draw zones in the editor: click to split, drag a line to resize, drop one on a neighbour to merge.
  • Start from twenty common shapes or a blank layout. Name layouts and zones, set a gap per layout.
  • Switch layouts from the menu bar. Every desktop of a display can use its own.

Stacks and the pill

  • Icons, names or both, along the top or bottom of the zone, always or only when you point at it.
  • Choose which app opens in which zone and open them all with one click.
  • A window dragged out of its zone glides back to the size it had.

Zones beyond the screen

  • Any number of areas past each edge, one after the other.
  • Hold keys and scroll with two fingers or the mouse wheel; a window that gets focus brings its zone into view.
  • Trackpad speed is a setting.

Everywhere

  • Several displays and desktops. Unplug a display and its apps move to the main screen; plug it in and they go back.
  • Closed, hidden and minimized windows leave their zone and return when they do.
  • Updates from inside the app. 46 languages.

Install

PaneFlip asks for two permissions on the first launch, Accessibility and Screen Recording, and tells you why.

From a download

Open the .dmg of the latest release and drag PaneFlip into Applications. The app is signed with its own certificate, not an Apple developer one: the first time, open it, then choose System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Open Anyway.

Get the latest release

With Homebrew

Homebrew removes the quarantine mark, so no extra step is needed.

brew install --cask ffferitkartalll/tap/paneflipapp

Or take the quarantine mark off by hand:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/PaneFlip.app
